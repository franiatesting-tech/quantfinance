## Audit Conclusion

Current results are solid as descriptive historical research for a real-data 10-stock universe, especially for returns, drawdowns, simple VaR/ES, correlation, basic portfolio concentration and strict ML OOS gates. They are partial for broker-grade inference because risk-free data failed, factor-adjusted alpha is unavailable, multiple-testing controls are incomplete, covariance estimation is noisy, execution costs are uncalibrated and no paper/live broker integration should be enabled.

The final iteration should implement robust diagnostics that do not require external proprietary data: covariance shrinkage comparison, VaR backtesting, multiple-testing approximations, model-confidence status, portfolio robustness, factor-model scaffolding, execution-cost sensitivity, final research-only gates, deterministic final paper generation and reproducibility manifests.
